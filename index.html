Who we are

The Art of Science

Our department advances the art of science, inspired by problems arising in engineering and the physical, biological, information and social sciences. Since 1988, we've been producing statisticians who take on the big challenges of a data-driven world — and the new ones still emerging.

  • Undergraduate & postgraduate programmes in Statistics
  • Strong research culture in biostatistics, data mining & statistical learning
  • Modern labs and software training (R, Python, SPSS, SAS)
  • Alumni in academia, industry, banking, health & development sectors

Programmes

Choose Your Path

From first-year foundations to doctoral research, our ladder of programmes takes you from learning statistics to creating it.

BSc

B.Sc. Honours in Statistics

4-year undergraduate · 80 seats

A rigorous four-year degree covering probability, inference, linear models, sampling, experimental design, econometrics, demography and statistical computing.

4 years · 8 semesters Learn more →
MS

MS in Statistics

Postgraduate · 18-month

Advanced coursework plus a thesis option. Specialise in biostatistics, applied econometrics, statistical learning or design of experiments — mentored by active researchers.
