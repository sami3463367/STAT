Our story

About the Department

Thirty-eight years of turning questions into data, and data into answers — in the heart of Dinajpur.

Mission & Vision

Why We Exist

Our goal is nothing less than a world-class statistics department, providing the highest quality education and leading-edge research.

Our Mission

Educate · Analyse · Inspire

To deliver excellence in statistics education and research, producing graduates equipped with strong theoretical foundations and practical data skills. We advance the art of science, inspired by problems arising in engineering and the physical, biological, information and social sciences.

Our Vision

A World-Class Statistics Department

To be a leading centre of statistical learning and research in Bangladesh — a department where theory meets practice, where every student learns to see the story hidden in the numbers, and where research responds to the real needs of agriculture, health, economy and society.

History

From Canvas to Mural

A timeline of the department and its parent institution in northern Bangladesh.

1979

The institution begins as the Agricultural Extension Training Institute (AETI), awarding a three-year diploma in agriculture — the seed from which HSTU grows.

1988

AETI is upgraded to Hajee Mohammad Danesh Agricultural College, affiliated with Bangladesh Agricultural University, Mymensingh. The Department of Statistics is established the same year.

1999

The college is upgraded to university status as Hajee Mohammad Danesh Science and Technology University. The first batch of students is admitted for the 1999–2000 session.

Today

The Department of Statistics offers B.Sc. (Hons.), MS and PhD programmes, with faculty conducting research from statistical theory to big data, biostatistics and machine learning.

Chairman's Message

Welcome to the Wall

Welcome for research and education to the Department of Statistics at Hajee Mohammad Danesh Science and Technology University, Dinajpur, Bangladesh. The field of statistics is one of the most important disciplines in our modern data-driven world — big data decides what we watch and listen to, medical advances come from statistically designed trials, and climate and financial forecasts rest on statistical methods.

There is growing demand for highly skilled statisticians in the 21st century, and our department is producing the professionals who can take on these challenges — and the new ones that will emerge.

Why statistics?

Every Field Needs a Measurer
