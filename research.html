Featured work

In the Spotlight

A sample of the applied statistical research conducted by our faculty — especially in the areas where Bangladesh needs answers most.

Agriculture · Efficiency

Technical Change & Productive Inefficiency of Wheat Farming in Bangladesh

A stochastic frontier production-function analysis of wheat farming — published in the International Journal of Statistical Sciences. The work measures how farmers convert inputs into output and identifies where efficiency gains are possible.

Project lead: Prof. Dr. Md. Earfan Ali Khondaker

Education · Analytics

Factors Affecting Academic Achievement of Students

An interdisciplinary study by our faculty (Dilara Pervin, Sabuj Ali, A.S.M. Abu Saeed, Kaderi Kibria and colleagues) on the determinants of student performance — evidence that statistics can improve how we teach.

Published: International Journal of Statistics & Applied Mathematics · 2025

Capacity Building · HEQEP

Statistical Data Mining for Agriculture, Bioinformatics & Environment

A World Bank HEQEP / UGC academic innovation project that modernised teaching and syllabuses around statistical data mining — preparing graduates for bioinformatics, climatology and health analytics.

Funded: Higher Education Quality Enhancement Project (HEQEP) · AIF
