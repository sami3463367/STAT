Academic Programmes

A complete ladder from the first probability class to doctoral-level original research.

Degrees offered

One Wall, Three Doors

All programmes are taught in English, aligned with UGC curriculum standards and delivered by research-active faculty.

Undergraduate

B.Sc. (Honours) in Statistics

A four-year, eight-semester honours programme (with a one-year MS extension) that builds a complete foundation in probability, theoretical and applied statistics, plus the computing skills modern data work demands. Around 80 students are admitted each year.

Illustrative course structure (semesters differ by year)
YearCore areasComputing & practice
1st YearProbability, Descriptive Statistics, Calculus & Algebra, MicroeconomicsIntro to Statistical Software
2nd YearStatistical Inference, Probability Distributions, Sampling, Linear AlgebraData Handling Lab
3rd YearDesign of Experiments, Regression & Correlation, Demography, Applied EconometricsR / SPSS / Python Labs
4th YearMultivariate Analysis, Time Series, Non-parametric Methods, Statistical Quality ControlResearch Project & Thesis

Postgraduate

Master of Science (MS) in Statistics

An advanced programme for graduates seeking depth in statistical theory and modern applications. Students complete advanced coursework and a research thesis or project, choosing specialisations guided by faculty expertise.

📊

Core

Advanced inference, linear models, stochastic processes, multivariate methods.

🧬

Elective A

Biostatistics, epidemiology, clinical trial design, survival analysis.

🤖

Elective B

Data mining, statistical learning, machine learning, big-data analytics.

Doctoral

Doctor of Philosophy (PhD)

Original, publishable research under the supervision of our faculty. Current doctoral research spans statistical theory, stochastic analysis, econometrics, biostatistics, data mining and statistical learning — and cross-disciplinary collaborations across the university.

  • Supervision by active researchers in diverse statistical fields
  • Access to modern computing facilities and datasets
  • Opportunities for national and international collaboration
Admission

How to Join

Admissions follow the university-wide HSTU admission process. Here's the general route for the current cycle.

Step 01

Apply Online

Submit the online application through the HSTU admission portal when the notice is published.

Step 02

Admission Test

Qualify through the university's central admission test; the Faculty of Science unit determines merit.

Step 03

Merit List

Selected candidates appear on the merit list — around 80 seats in B.Sc. (Hons.) Statistics each year.

Step 04

Enrol

Complete registration and orientation. Your data story officially begins!
